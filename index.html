About Our Institute

Learn more about our history, mission and commitments

Main Academic Block, TechNova Campus

Committed to Quality Technical Education

TechNova Institute Of Engineering was established in the year 1998 with the aim of providing high-quality technical education to students from across the country. Over the past two decades, the college has established itself as a reputable center for undergraduate and postgraduate education.

The institute is approved by AICTE, New Delhi and affiliated with the State Technical University. Spread over a green 25-acre campus, we provide a peaceful learning atmosphere with well-ventilated classrooms, computer labs, workshop areas, and sports facilities.

Our Vision

To emerge as a premier technical institute recognized for academic excellence, ethical values, and industry readiness.

Our Mission

To provide student-centric learning, promote innovation, develop professional skills, and prepare graduates for successful careers.

"Our aim is not just to prepare students for examinations, but to prepare them with practical skills and discipline required for the professional world."

— Prof. (Dr.) S. K. Verma, Principal & Director

Campus Facilities

Essential infrastructure to support student learning and campus life

Central Library

Over 45,000 text and reference books, subscription to national journals, magazines, and a quiet reading hall.

Computer Laboratories

Air-conditioned labs with 500+ updated PCs, high-speed Wi-Fi internet, and licensed software packages for student practice.

Mechanical & Civil Workshops

Dedicated workshops for carpentry, fitting, welding, foundry, and structural materials testing.

Sports & Gymnasium

Playgrounds for cricket, football, volleyball, and basketball, along with indoor table tennis, chess, and fitness gym.

Hostel Accommodation

Separate secure hostels for boys and girls with furnished rooms, mess facilities, RO drinking water, and round-the-clock wardens.

Cafeteria

Hygienic campus canteen serving affordable breakfast, lunch, snacks, tea, and beverages throughout college hours.
